**NHWA indicator 1-07: Health worker distribution by place of birth.** Percentage of active health workers by their place of birth

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers by place of birth | Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers by their place of birth. Place of birth is defined as national-born or foreign-born. This indicator will capture the information on the health workers coming from abroad.

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Professional council/chamber/association registers; Health facility data; Population census data.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: WHO Global Code of Practice on the International Recruitment of Health Personnel, Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-birth-history`, `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-citizenship-and-language`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-07 (page 58), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-07`, for working-group review.
