**NHWA indicator 2-05: Duration of education and training.** Duration of health workforce education and training programmes, in years

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | By health workforce education and training programme |

*Definition:* Duration of health workforce education and training is the number of years required to complete a full curriculum for each health workforce education and training programme. This duration does not include specializations or time to obtain additional, optional, certifications.

*Potential data sources:* Ministry of Education; Database on education and training statistics; Education and training institutions.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM).

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 2-05 (page 70), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.2-05`, for working-group review.
