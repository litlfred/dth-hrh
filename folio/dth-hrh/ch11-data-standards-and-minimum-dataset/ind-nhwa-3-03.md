**NHWA indicator 3-03: Total expenditure on health workforce education.** Total expenditure on health workforce pre-service education (current and capital)

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | By health workforce education and training programme, by health workforce education and training programme and institution ownership (public/private) |

*Definition:* Total expenditure in US$ on health workforce education (current and capital). The disaggregation enables to differentiate between public vs private sources and distribution across programmes. Education here refers to pre-service education and does not include in-service education and training.

*Potential data sources:* Integrated Financial Management Information System; Ministry of Finance; Ministry of Health; Ministry of Education; Ministry of Defence (expenditure on military HWF education may fall under the Ministry of Defence budget); Education Management Information System: Ministry of (Higher) Education; ministry or other accredited bodies responsible for technical and vocational education and training; National Bureau of Statistics – Government financial statistics departments; System of Health Accounts; National Education Accounts.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM).

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 3-03 (page 78), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.3-03`, for working-group review.
