Governance candidate

Governance is the set of arrangements through which decision authority is distributed, exercised and accounted for across all those who depend upon, contribute to, or are served by the shared infrastructure.

Also called: governance, governance (health supply chain)

RA §3.4.1 body wording, verbatim (library/who-dpi-h-reference-architecture-draft-v1/sections/sec-029-341-purpose-and-framing.md, p. 64–65). Substantive alternative in SC handbook Ch. 3 boxed definition (who-iris/library/9789240101197-eng/sections/page-045.md, p. 45): "In the context of the health supply chain, governance is the process of establishing and implementing policies, procedures and mechanisms to ensure that the supply chain processes and the systems to manage those processes are managed effectively, ethically and in alignment with established objectives and stakeholders’ interests." (RA defines governance as the distribution, exercise, and accountability of decision authority across shared infrastructure; SC defines it as the process of establishing and implementing policies, procedures, and mechanisms for supply chain processes and systems.) Also related to `digital-health-governance` in Mehl et al. 2023 (glossary/full-stac.glossary.json, smart-ra#143 P8). Also framed in SFP handbook §2.3 (who-iris/library/9789240120747-eng/sections/sec-015-23-establish-governance-and-leadership.md, page-028.md, p. 16) as a structure for coordinating multiple stakeholders, defining clear roles and responsibilities, enhancing transparency, and creating a stable platform for decision-making. Both definitions are recorded as candidates; neither is chosen (smart-ra#133 S1, smart-ra#140, dth-sfp#1 P2).
