**Purpose.** This table decides a practitioner's new registration and licence status after a lifecycle event (renewal, expiry, suspension, revocation, reinstatement, employment update, retirement, emigration or death), always keeping the earlier states. The registration officer uses it when recording such an event (processes HRH-BP-01 and HRH-BP-03). It serves the lifecycle requirements [Manage status changes](#freq:FR-LC-01) and [Support renewals, recording of continuing professional development](#freq:FR-LC-02).

Decision table **HRH-DT-02** (the first matching rule applies) formalizes the rules in [**Section 7.3 (Lifecycle management)**](#sec:7-3) and [**Section 4.3 (Health worker lifecycle)**](#sec:4-3) for transitioning a practitioner's registration and licence status across renewal, employment updates, suspension, revocation, reinstatement, retirement/emigration and death ([Manage status changes](#freq:FR-LC-01)), while preserving all historical states without overwriting prior records ([Support renewals, recording of continuing professional development](#freq:FR-LC-02)).
