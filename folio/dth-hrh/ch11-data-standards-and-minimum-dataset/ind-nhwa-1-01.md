**NHWA indicator 1-01: Health worker density.** Density of health workers per 10 000 population

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | Total population | By occupation, by occupation and activity level |

*Definition:* Number of health workers per 10 000 population. For activity level the following categories are recommended: practising health workers, professionally active health workers, and health workers licensed to practise. Total population as estimated in the World Population Prospects published by the Population Division of the Department of Economic and Social Affairs of the United Nations Secretariat. In case of other methodology used, WHO recalculates densities according to the UN population estimates in order to harmonize the densities and ensure comparability.

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Aggregate data from health facilities (routine administrative records, Health Management Information System, District Health Information System census and/or survey); Professional council/chamber/association registers; Labour force surveys; Population census data; United Nations Population Division.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: UN SDG 3.c.1, SDG 3.8.1, WHO Global Code of Practice on the International Recruitment of Health Personnel, Global Strategy for Human Resources for Health: Workforce 2030 (GSHRH), Working for Health programme, Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), Immunization Agenda 2030 (IA2030), SCORE for Health Data Technical Package (SCORE), Primary Health Care (PHC) Measurement Framework, Community Health Workers (CHWs), Global Oral Health Action Plan.

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-employment-status`, `de:nhwa-m1-activity-level`, `de:cde-professional-licence-and-certification`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-01 (page 52), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-01`, for working-group review.
