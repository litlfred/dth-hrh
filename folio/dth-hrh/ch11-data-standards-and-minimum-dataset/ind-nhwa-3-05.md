**NHWA indicator 3-05: Average tuition fee per student.** Average annual tuition fee per student enrolled in health workforce education and training

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Total tuition fees paid by students enrolled in health workforce education and training programmes for a given year | Total number of students enrolled in health workforce education and training programmes | By health workforce education and training programme |

*Definition:* Average tuition fee in US$ per student enrolled in health workforce education and training per year, by health workforce education and training programme. Whenever possible, this indicator should exclude students exonerated from paying tuition fees.

*Potential data sources:* Ministry of Finance; Ministry of Education; Databases on education statistics; Education and training institutions.

*Reporting:* Annual.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 3-05 (page 80), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.3-05`, for working-group review.
