Proposed core and optional data elements with definitions: the core data elements for a health workforce registry (CDE-HRH). CDE-HRH takes inspiration from the WHO 2015 minimum data set.

This handbook uses "core data elements" and CDE-HRH rather than "minimum data set" or MDS-HRH, because the older term has caused confusion and is not precise. The title of the WHO 2015 publication is cited as published.

The 2015 elements are drafted as structured data-element blocks in section 11.1.
