**NHWA indicator 1-05: Health worker distribution by facility ownership.** Percentage of active health workers employed by facility ownership

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers, defined in headcounts, working in facilities owned by the given institutional sector (public or private not-for-profit or private for-profit) | Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers employed by facility ownership (public, private not-for-profit, private for-profit). The categories of facility ownership can be aligned to institutional sector definitions of the System of National Accounts (SNA 2008).

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Aggregate data from health facilities (routine administrative records, Health Management Information System, District Health Information System census and/or survey); Labour force surveys; Census.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: SCORE for Health Data Technical Package (SCORE).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-employment-address`, `de:nhwa-m1-facility-ownership`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-05 (page 56), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-05`, for working-group review.
