**NHWA indicator 3-02: Entry-level wages and salaries.** Average entry-level wage and salary excluding social contributions

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | By occupation, by occupation and public/private |

*Definition:* Average wage or salary in US$ received by health workers when entering the active health labour market, excluding social contributions. Disaggregation by public vs private source is suggested, although often only the source from the public sector is available.

*Potential data sources:* Ministry of Health records; Ministry of Finance records; Payroll data; Income tax data; General labour force surveys; Specific health worker surveys.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:nhwa-m3-employer-sector`, `de:nhwa-m3-entry-salary-grade`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 3-02 (page 77), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.3-02`, for working-group review.
