**NHWA indicator 1-12: Health worker distribution by type of contract.** Percentage of active health workers by their type of contract

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers in specific types of contracts (full-time vs part-time) | Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers in the given type of contract. Type of contract are defined as either full-time or part-time. • Full-time workers are defined as employed or self-employed persons whose normal working hours in their main job correspond at least to the legal number of working hours per week, i.e. usually working 30 hours per week or above. • Part-time workers are defined as employed or self-employed persons whose normal hours of work are less than those of comparable full-time workers.

*Potential data sources:* Facility database and/or surveys; Health workforce registry or database; Labour force surveys; Public service human resources and payroll administrations.

*Reporting:* Annual.

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:nhwa-m1-type-of-contract`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-12 (page 63), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-12`, for working-group review.
