**NHWA indicator 2-07: Standards for education and training programmes.** Existence of standards for education and training programmes

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | By health workforce education and training programme (for all but last sub-indicator 2-07.7) |

*Definition:* This indicator is a composite of seven sub-indicators, see below. The response to these seven sub-indicators is aggregated as a score (see part II on methods) on a scale from 1 (nascent) to 5 (sustainable). Each sub-indicator is a self-assessment of one aspect of standards on education and training programmes. To help answer these self-assessed sub-indicators, a series of guiding questions are provided.

*Sub-indicators:*

- 2-07.1 Existence of national and/or subnational standards for social accountability in accreditation mechanisms of training programmes (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.2 Existence of national and/or subnational standards for the social determinants of health in accreditation mechanisms of training programmes (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.3 Existence of national and/or subnational standards for interprofessional education in accreditation mechanisms of training programmes (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.4 Existence of cooperation between health workforce education and training institutions and regulatory bodies to agree on accreditation standards (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.5 Existence of national systems for continuing professional development (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.6 Existence of in-service training as an element of national education plans for the health workforce (Yes/Partial/No)
- 2-07.7 Existence of national and/or subnational standards for community health workers’ curriculum (Yes/ Partial/No/Not applicable)

*Potential data sources:* Ministry of Health; Ministries of Education, Higher Education or similar; National accreditation authorities; Legitimate bodies, statutory corporations; Professional council/chamber/association registers; Ministries responsible for labour.

*Reporting:* Every three years. Frameworks: Global Strategy for Human Resources for Health: Workforce 2030 (GSHRH), Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), Primary Health Care (PHC) Measurement Framework, Community Health Workers (CHWs).

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 2-07 (pages 72-73), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.2-07`, for working-group review.
