**NHWA indicator 1-11: Vacancy rate.** Ratio of unfilled posts to total number of funded posts

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of funded full-time posts that have not been filled for at least twelve months | Total number of funded full-time posts (filled and unfilled) | By occupation |

*Definition:* Ratio of unfilled posts to total number of posts. By limiting the count of unfilled posts for at least twelve months, this indicator enables to distinguish between a systematic job vacancy situation versus potential rapid turnover of staff.

*Potential data sources:* Labour force surveys; Health facility assessments; Employment offices and/or job agencies.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), SCORE for Health Data Technical Package (SCORE).

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-11 (page 62), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-11`, for working-group review.
