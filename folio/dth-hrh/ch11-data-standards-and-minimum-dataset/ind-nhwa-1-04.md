**NHWA indicator 1-04: Health worker distribution by sex.** Percentage of active health workers by sex

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers in sex categories | Total number of active male and female health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers in the given sex category. Sex group corresponds to male or female health workers.

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Aggregate data from health facilities (routine administrative records, Health Management Information System, District Health Information System census and/or survey); Labour force surveys; Population census data.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: UN SDG 3.c.1, Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), SCORE for Health Data Technical Package (SCORE).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-sex`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-04 (page 55), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-04`, for working-group review.
