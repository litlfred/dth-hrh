**Purpose.** This table decides what happens to a registration application: register and license, hold it for duplicate review or pending verification, or reject it. The registration officer at the regulatory body uses it when processing a new application (processes HRH-BP-01 and HRH-BP-02). It serves the identity and records requirements [Assign a unique, persistent health worker identifier](#freq:FR-ID-01), [Search, match and merge duplicate records across source systems](#freq:FR-ID-02), [Create, view, update and archive the health worker record…](#freq:FR-REC-01) and [Hold supporting evidence, such as certificates](#freq:FR-REC-02).

Decision table **HRH-DT-01** (the first matching rule applies) formalizes the rules in [**Section 7.1 (Identity management)**](#sec:7-1) and [**Section 7.2 (Records)**](#sec:7-2) governing when a registration application creates a new persistent health worker identifier ([Assign a unique, persistent health worker identifier](#freq:FR-ID-01)), reuses an existing identifier across cadres or specialties, holds a candidate duplicate for manual adjudication ([Search, match and merge duplicate records across source systems](#freq:FR-ID-02)), or rejects an application lacking primary-source qualification attestation ([Create, view, update and archive the health worker record…](#freq:FR-REC-01), [Hold supporting evidence, such as certificates](#freq:FR-REC-02)).

*Source passages cited:* [Identity management](#prose:ch07-identity-management), [Assign a unique, persistent health worker identifier](#freq:FR-ID-01), [Search, match and merge duplicate records across source systems](#freq:FR-ID-02), [Create, view, update and archive the health worker record…](#freq:FR-REC-01), [Hold supporting evidence, such as certificates](#freq:FR-REC-02), and [Register and license a new graduate](#scen:register-and-license-new-graduate). Qualitative states come strictly from the source text; no numeric matching scores or day thresholds are invented.
