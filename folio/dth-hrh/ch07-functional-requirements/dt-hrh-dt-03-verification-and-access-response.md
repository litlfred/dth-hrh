**Purpose.** This table decides how much of a health worker's record to disclose when someone asks for it: public status only, an operational view for authorized employers and clinical systems, de-identified aggregates for planners, the full record for the health worker or the custodian, or nothing. The registration officer and the registry apply it to verification and data requests (processes HRH-BP-03 and HRH-BP-05), under the disclosure policy the governance body approves (HRH-BP-06). It serves the verification, analytics and access requirements [Confirm registration and licence status in real time for…](#freq:FR-VER-01), [Issue digital, verifiable credentials or certificates (for example a…](#freq:FR-VER-02), [Respond to verification requests from other countries](#freq:FR-VER-03), [Data quality reports for registry managers](#freq:FR-AN-03), [Record the source and date of each data item](#freq:FR-REC-03) and [Security](#nfreq:NFR-01).

Decision table **HRH-DT-03** (the first matching rule applies) formalizes the rules in [**Section 7.4 (Verification services)**](#sec:7-4), [**Section 7.5 (Analytics)**](#sec:7-5), [**Section 6.2 (Legal foundations)**](#sec:6-2) and [**Section 6.3 (Data stewardship)**](#sec:6-3) for tiered disclosure when responding to status verification and data access requests across public lookup ([Confirm registration and licence status in real time for…](#freq:FR-VER-01)), system-to-system clinical/HR verification ([Issue digital, verifiable credentials or certificates (for example a…](#freq:FR-VER-02)), online/offline verifiable credentials ([Respond to verification requests from other countries](#freq:FR-VER-03)), de-identified analytics extracts ([Data quality reports for registry managers](#freq:FR-AN-03)), and self/stewardship record inspection ([Record the source and date of each data item](#freq:FR-REC-03), [Security](#nfreq:NFR-01)).
