**NHWA indicator 3-01: Expenditure on compensation of health workers.** Total expenditure on compensation of health workers

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | *not printed in the NHWA handbook; for the working group* | By public/private |

*Definition:* Total expenditure expressed in US$ on compensation of health workers. If possible, disaggregation by public or private sources for the compensation of health workers. Sometimes information is only available for public expenditure on compensation of health workers.

*Potential data sources:* Ministry of Health records; Ministry of Finance records; Public/social health insurance; System of health accounts.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:nhwa-m3-employer-sector`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 3-01 (page 76), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.3-01`, for working-group review.
