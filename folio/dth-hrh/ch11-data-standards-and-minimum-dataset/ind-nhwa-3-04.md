**NHWA indicator 3-04: Expenditure per graduate on health workforce education.** Expenditure per graduate of health workforce education and training programme

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Total expenditure on health workforce education | Total number of graduates from health workforce education and training programmes | By health workforce education and training programme, by health workforce education and training programme and institution ownership (public/private) |

*Definition:* Expenditure in US$ on health workforce education per graduate. The disaggregation by programme enables to identify inequities across programmes. The disaggregation by institution ownership enables to distinguish between publicly and privately owned education institutions.

*Potential data sources:* Integrated Financial Management Information System: Ministry of Finance; Ministry of Health; Ministry of Education; Ministry of Defence (expenditure on military HWF education may fall under this budget); Education Management Information System: Ministry of (Higher) Education; ministry or other accredited body responsible for technical and vocational education and training; National Bureau of Statistics – Government financial statistics departments; System of Health Accounts; National Education Accounts.

*Reporting:* Annual.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 3-04 (page 79), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.3-04`, for working-group review.
