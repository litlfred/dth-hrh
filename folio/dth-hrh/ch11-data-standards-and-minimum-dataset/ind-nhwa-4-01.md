**NHWA indicator 4-01: Labour regulations and policies for health workforce.** Existence of labour regulations and policies on the employment characteristics, protection and working conditions of the health workforce

*Definition:* This indicator is a composite of twelve sub-indicators, see below. The response to these twelve sub-indicators is aggregated as a score (see part II on methods) on a scale from 1 (nascent) to 5 (sustainable). Each sub-indicator is a self-assessment of one aspect of labour regulation and policies, primarily to protect and safeguard health and care workers. To help answer these self-assessed sub-indicators, a series of guiding questions are provided.

*Sub-indicators:*

- 4-01.1 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws regulating working hours and conditions (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.2 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws regulating minimum wage (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.3 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws regulating social protection (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.4 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws regulating dual practice (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.5 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws regulating compulsory service (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.6 Existence of national/subnational policies/laws for prevention of attacks on health workers (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.7 Existence of national/subnational care packages for mental well-being of health workers (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.8 Existence of mechanisms for in-kind renumeration to promote rural retention (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.9 Existence of regulatory mechanisms for promoting health worker safety (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.10 Existence of regulatory mechanisms to ensure oversight of the activities of health workers within the private sector (Yes/Partial/No)
- 4-01.11 Existence of remuneration of community health workers through salary (Yes/Partial/No/Not applicable)
- 4-01.12 Existence of advanced nursing roles (Yes/Partial/No)

*Potential data sources:* Employment laws, policies and regulations; Social security records; Government or legislative records; Survey among country experts or informants; Policy and strategic documents of governments and competent authorities.

*Reporting:* Every three years. Frameworks: Global Strategy for Human Resources for Health: Workforce 2030 (GSHRH), Working for Health programme, Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), Global Health and Care Worker Compact, Community Health Workers (CHWs).

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 4-01 (pages 82-84), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.4-01`, for working-group review.
