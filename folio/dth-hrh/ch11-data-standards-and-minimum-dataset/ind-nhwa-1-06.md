**NHWA indicator 1-06: Health worker distribution by facility type.** Percentage of active health workers employed by facility type

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers, defined in headcounts, working in a specific facility type | Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers employed in the given facility type. Health facility types are based on the classification of System of Health Accounts: • Hospitals (HP.1) • Residential long-term care facilities (HP.2) • Providers of ambulatory health care (HP.3) • Ancillary services (HP.4, including transportation, emergency rescue, laboratories and others) • Retailers (HP.5, including pharmacies) • Providers of preventive care (HP.6)

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Aggregate data from health facilities (routine administrative records, Health Management Information System, District Health Information System census and/or survey); Labour Force Surveys; Census.

*Reporting:* Annual.

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-employment-address`, `de:nhwa-m1-facility-type`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-06 (page 57), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-06`, for working-group review.
