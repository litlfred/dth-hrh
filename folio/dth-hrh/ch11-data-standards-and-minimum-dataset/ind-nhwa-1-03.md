**NHWA indicator 1-03: Health worker distribution by age group.** Percentage of active health workers in different age groups

| Numerator | Denominator | Disaggregation |
|---|---|---|
| Number of active health workers in age group categories | Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts | By occupation, by occupation and sex |

*Definition:* Percentage of active health workers in the given age and sex category. This indicator enables to create the population pyramid of health workers. Age groups considered are the following: < 25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64, ≥ 65 years. Sex groups corresponds to male or female health workers.

*Potential data sources:* Health workforce registry or database; Aggregate data from health facilities (routine administrative records, Health Management Information System, District Health Information System census and/or survey); Professional council/chamber/association registers; Labour force surveys; Population census data.

*Reporting:* Annual. Frameworks: UN SDG 3.c.1, Global Strategic Directions for Nursing and Midwifery (SDNM), SCORE for Health Data Technical Package (SCORE).

*Data elements in this chapter:* `de:cde-birth-history`, `de:cde-occupation`, `de:cde-sex`.

*Source:* National health workforce accounts: a handbook, 2nd edition, indicator 1-03 (page 54), word for word, from the ingested L1 node in who-iris. Draft L2 indicator `NHWA.1-03`, for working-group review.
